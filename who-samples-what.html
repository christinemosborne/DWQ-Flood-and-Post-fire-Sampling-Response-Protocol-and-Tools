How partners collaborate without duplicating effort

  1. Confirm roles at the first interagency call (by 24 hours). Walk through this table, name a sampling lead and a backup for each agency, and record them in the response roster.
  2. Register every planned sampling trip before going out. Enter the site, date, agency and parameters on the plan board in the Shared Sampling Data Hub. The board flags any other agency planning the same site within a day.
  3. One agency per site per round. When two plans overlap, one agency samples and the other moves to an uncovered site or adds its parameters to the same trip. Split samples happen only by agreement, for QA comparisons.
  4. Use shared site IDs. DWQ monitoring location IDs and USGS station numbers; a new site gets an ID before its data are shared.
  5. Share results on a set clock. Upload preliminary results to the hub within 72 hours, marked preliminary; replace them with final, validated results when they’re available. Official records still go to each agency’s own system (AWQMS and WQX for DWQ).
  6. Review coverage weekly. The weekly call goes over the plan board, gaps (uses or reaches nobody is covering) and the next round.
  7. Speak with one voice. Results that may lead to an advisory go to the health department and the communications lead before anyone posts them (Risk Communication Plan).
